How enquiries work

Simple enough to feel reassuring.

  1. Enquiry

    Share the work type, property context, timing and any useful notes.

  2. Initial review

    PandaZen reviews the details and decides whether further discussion or a site visit is useful.

  3. Quotation

    The work is scoped clearly, including assumptions and boundaries where appropriate.

  4. Scheduled work

    Work is organised around the home, with care for access, protection and communication.

  5. Completion

    The finished work is checked for detail, presentation and practical use.
