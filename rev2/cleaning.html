Quiet professionalism for busy households.

PandaZen Cleaning is a boutique domestic cleaning service for homes that need reliability, care and consistency. The approach is personal and traditional: clear communication, punctual visits, respectful work and high standards every time.

It is a small, owner-led service built around long-term trust rather than rotating visits. The aim is a calmer home and a cleaning routine that becomes familiar, discreet and dependable.

Continuity

A familiar standard, visit after visit.

Care

Respect for surfaces, products and the way you live.

Professional products can be supplied, or preferred client products used. Product preferences, delicate areas and surface care are discussed so the clean suits the home.

Trust
